CZEDR — iPhone login (simple steps)
=====================================

1) On your PC, double-click this file:
   
   scripts\START-IPHONE-TESTING.cmd

   • When Windows asks for permission, click YES.
   • Leave the black window OPEN while you use the app.

2) On your iPhone (same Wi‑Fi as the PC), open Safari and go to the
   address shown in that window, for example:

   http://192.168.68.51:8080/v1/health

   You should see text starting with {"Status":"true"

   If Safari does NOT load that page, the PC firewall is still blocking
   the phone. Run scripts\allow-lan-api-firewall.cmd (right‑click →
   Run as administrator) and click YES. This is required when your Wi‑Fi
   is set to "Public" in Windows.

3) In the Czedr app on iPhone (build 46+):
   • The app finds your PC on Wi‑Fi automatically (wait for "found automatically").
   • You can still type the API address from step 2 if needed (without /v1/health).
   • Email:    alice@test.czedr
   • Password: TestPass1234!

   After sign-in you may be asked to create a 4-digit PIN. Enter the same
   4 digits twice (e.g. 1234 and 1234 again), then tap the save button.

4) iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network → Czedr → ON

5) Rebuild/run the app from Xcode on your Mac if you changed the project
   recently (your developer does this once).

Need help? Tell support what happens in step 2 (Safari works or not).
