About CZEDR

CZEDR is a simple way to send and receive payments. Sign in, set your PIN, and pay by Czedr ID — on your phone, from anywhere.

  • Secure HTTPS API at api.czedr.com
  • Micro-deposit bank linking (no aggregator logins)
  • Pay from your balance — no card numbers at checkout
