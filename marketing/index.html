Get the app on iPhone

Install Czedr through Apple TestFlight.

  1. Install Apple’s TestFlight app from the App Store (free) if you don’t have it yet.
  2. Open TestFlight on your iPhone and tap Czedr, or use your invite link.
  3. Sign in with your Czedr account — the app uses api.czedr.com.

Need an invite? Email support@czedr.com

About CZEDR

CZEDR is a simple way to send and receive payments. Sign in, set your PIN, and pay by Czedr ID — on your phone, from anywhere.

  • Secure HTTPS API at api.czedr.com
  • Micro-deposit bank linking (no aggregator logins)
  • Pay from your balance — no card numbers at checkout
